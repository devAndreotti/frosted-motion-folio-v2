A few months ago I came across a YouTube video called "Simulating Black Holes in C++"

The video: https://lnkd.in/dp9keFMf

Back then it still had very few views. I remember watching it and thinking: "someday I want to play with this."

I saved the video and moved on.

Months later it came back to mind. I looked up the repository again, forked it and started experimenting.

What was just curiosity ended up turning into a much bigger project than I had imagined.

Along the way I explored new ideas and added features such as:

- Planets and scenery
- Cinematic rendering
- A live wallpaper mode for Windows
- And a terminal renderer that runs exactly the same simulation on the GPU (the best part)

More than shipping features, this project became a great lab for studying computer graphics, shaders and real-time rendering.

That is exactly the kind of thing I love most about open source.

You find an interesting idea, learn how it works, experiment, tweak it, create new possibilities... and, in the end, give something different back to the community.

None of this would exist without the foundation built by kavan010.

Many thanks to him for making this project available. Building on top of it was an incredibly fun experience.
