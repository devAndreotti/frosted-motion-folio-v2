Scams are not just system vulnerabilities. They rely on haste, fear, and someone who sounds trustworthy on the other end of the line.

I completed a University Extension activity in the Computer Science program at Universidade Paulista, focused on community digital protection. The deliverables are a practical guide booklet and an infographic on the topic:

Digital Shield & Practical protection against Pix scams, fake WhatsApp, and email tracking. The activity is aligned with two United Nations Sustainable Development Goals:

• SDG 10 :: Reduced Inequalities
• SDG 16 :: Peace, Justice, and Strong Institutions

The material covers:

• Fake bank call centers and essential checks before confirming any financial transaction;
• MED (Special Refund Mechanism) for Pix transfers and how to trigger it with your bank;
• Two-step verification on WhatsApp and best practices against account cloning;
• A first-response triage checklist in case fraud has already taken place.

📘 Guide booklet and infographic available for free in PDF: https://lnkd.in/dzWZ3yGZ
