AI already appears in triage, medical imaging analysis, and hospital operations. But when handling clinical data, innovation must advance alongside data privacy and the essential doctor-patient relationship.

I completed a University Extension project in the Computer Science program at Universidade Paulista (UNIP), in which I authored an ebook on the topic:

The Algorithmic Healthcare Frontier // Data protection, AI, and patient-centered human care

The project is aligned with two United Nations Sustainable Development Goals:

• SDG 3 :: Good Health and Well-Being
• SDG 9 :: Industry, Innovation, and Infrastructure

The material covers:

• AI as clinical diagnostic decision support, never as an outright replacement for clinical judgment;
• Anonymization and safeguarding sensitive data in electronic health records under article 11 of LGPD;
• Algorithmic bias in medical training datasets and indirect statistical discrimination;
• Model governance, explainability, and technical auditability of AI systems in healthcare;
